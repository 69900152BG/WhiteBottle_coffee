WhiteBottle Spider Chart

Seven metrics, eight stores. Outward means a higher observed value. Hover a store to isolate it, click to show or hide it.

    Each axis runs from 0 (lowest store) to 100 (highest store) across all eight stores, so shapes show where a store sits relative to the others, not an overall score. Complaints are shown per 1,000 transactions; higher means worse service, and promotion spend is a cost, so a larger shape is not a better store. Hover a dot to see the actual value.
